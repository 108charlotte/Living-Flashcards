Intro

Welcome to your first deck! Before you get started, let's learn how to use these flashcards.

First, you will be presented with a card. On the front, there will be a word or phrase in the language you selected to study.

When you see that phrase, try to translate it into English or a language you know.

If an audio button is present, you can also click it to hear the phrase pronounced by a native speaker.

Once you think you know what the phrase means, click the card to flip it over and check your guess.

Now, select one of the buttons based on how hard you found it to recall the card:

  • Again: you weren't able to come up with a translation, or yours was entirely off
  • Hard: you came up with a translation that was close or correct, but had to think hard to recall it
  • Good: you were able to come up with a correct translation in a reasonable amount of time
  • Easy: you quickly came up with the correct translation and think it's unlikely you'll forget it soon

Next to "Again", "Hard", "Good", and "Easy", you will also see a time (e.g. "< 10 mins") — this corresponds to when the next review will be scheduled.

Once you select an option, you will be presented with the next card! Check the progress bar above the deck to see how far you've gotten (green) and how much is left (gray).

Keyboard Shortcuts

1: Again  |  2: Hard  |  3: Good  |  4: Easy

Use the spacebar to flip the card back and forth.
