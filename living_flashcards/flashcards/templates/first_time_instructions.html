    Intro

    Welcome to your first deck! Before you get started, lets learn how to use these flashcards

    First, you will be presented with a card. On the front, there will be a word or phrase in the language you selected to study

    When you see that phrase, try to translate it into English or a language you know

    If an audio button is present, you can also click it to hear the phrase pronounced by a native speaker

    Once you think you know what the phrase means, click the card to flip it over and check your guess.

    Now, select one of the buttons based on how hard you found it to recall the card

    • Again: you weren't able to come up with a translation for the phrase, or yours was entirely off
    • Hard: you came up with a translation that was close to or exactly correct, but you had to think hard to recall it
    • Good: you were able to come up with a correct translation in a reasonable amount of time
    • Easy: you quickly came up with the correct translation and think its unlikely for you to forget it soon

    Next to the text saying "Again", "Hard", "Good", and "Easy", you will also see a time (ex. "< 10 mins").

    This corresponds to when the next review will be set

    Once you select an option, you will be presented with the next card! Check the progress bar above the deck to see how far you've gotten in the deck (green) and how much you have left to go (gray)
